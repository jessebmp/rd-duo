RD Duo is provided by Adjjective Limited Liability Company. This policy describes the iPhone/iPad app, Mac companion and this support site.

What the apps do

The Mac sends screen images, window and app information, and pointer state directly to a device you pair. The device sends control actions such as pointer movement, scrolling and typed text to the Mac. Pairing uses cryptographic identities stored locally in the devices’ keychains. The apps also keep local settings, paired-device information and connection addresses.

We do not operate an RD Duo account system or relay server. We do not receive or store your screen content, typed text or pairing secrets. The apps include no advertising or analytics SDK. Version 1 does not stream audio or synchronize clipboard content between devices.

Permissions

Screen Recording lets the Mac companion stream the screen you select. Accessibility lets it control your Mac. Local Network access discovers and connects to the Mac. Optional camera access scans a pairing code; the app does not upload camera images.

Diagnostics and third parties

Connection diagnostics are stored locally on your Mac. Review any log before sharing it. If you choose to send support information or post a public support issue, we receive the information you choose to share. Apple may provide crash reports when you opt into its diagnostic sharing or TestFlight testing.

The Mac companion checks GitHub for available updates when automatic checks are enabled. GitHub receives ordinary network request information such as your IP address. This website and public support issues are also hosted by GitHub and subject to GitHub’s privacy policy. A VPN you choose, such as Tailscale, is a separate service with its own policy.

Your choices

You can disconnect, unpair devices, turn off automatic update checks, revoke system permissions, or remove the apps. Local keychain pairing items can persist after uninstalling; use the apps’ device-removal controls before uninstalling if you want to revoke a pairing.

Contact and changes

For privacy questions, contact us through the support repository without posting private information. We will update this page when the product or its data handling changes.
