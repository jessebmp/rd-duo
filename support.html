Setup & support

From installed
to connected.

Start on the same Wi-Fi

  1. Download the free RD Duo for Mac companion and move it into Applications.
  2. Open it and allow Screen Recording and Accessibility in macOS Privacy & Security. Reopen the companion if macOS asks.
  3. Open RD Duo on your iPhone or iPad and allow Local Network access.
  4. Select your Mac. Enter or scan the pairing code that the Mac shows. Keep Require Pairing enabled.
  5. Move one finger to move the pointer, tap to click, use two fingers to scroll, and open the keyboard to type. A partly folded Duo uses the bottom half as the trackpad.

Connect away from home

Pair while you are near the Mac first. Set up a private VPN such as Tailscale on both devices and confirm that they can reach each other. In the Mac companion, enable Remote Access and Internet Access. Follow the companion’s address and connection status.

Your Mac must be powered, awake and signed in. A pairing code does not create a network route. Do not open a router port to make RD Duo work. Access before the first login after a restart, including the FileVault unlock screen, is not supported.

Mac not found?

Check that the companion is open, Local Network permission is allowed, and both devices are on the same reachable network. A guest Wi-Fi network can isolate devices. Check the VPN connection on both ends when away.

Black screen or pointer does not work?

Check Screen Recording and Accessibility for RD Duo for Mac. Reopen the companion after a permission change. Keep one copy in Applications. If the Mac has gone to sleep or restarted, someone may need to wake or sign in to it locally.

Clipboard and audio

Version 1 does not share the phone’s clipboard or stream Mac audio. Copy and Paste shortcuts operate on the Mac’s own clipboard.

Get help

Open a support issue with the app versions, device models and a short description of what happened. Issues are public: do not include pairing codes, private network addresses, screenshots of private content, credentials or unreviewed logs.
